About Proper Child Int’l

×

Established in 2011, Proper Child International School is a government-approved Christian institution in Etche LGA, Rivers State. We raise scholars of excellence, integrity, and global impact through faith, knowledge, and character.

Christian Values – rooted in truth and love
Global Standard – blended Nigerian & international curriculum
Excellence Culture – over a decade of academic distinction

Our Academic Programs

×

From Nursery to Secondary, we provide holistic education with modern laboratories, libraries, and ICT center. We groom leaders for tomorrow.

Nursery (Crèche - K.G.): Early years foundation, Montessori-inspired.
Primary (Grade 1-6): Phonics, Numeracy, STEM, Moral instruction.
Secondary (JSS1 - SSS3): Sciences, Arts, Commerce, Robotics club.
Extracurricular: Debate, Sports, Quiz, Christian Fellowship.

50 Etiquette Tips

×
1. Greet teachers and classmates warmly.
2. Say please and thank you.
3. Listen without interrupting.
4. Raise your hand before speaking.
5. Use kind and respectful words.
6. Keep your desk clean.
7. Hold the door for others.
8. Apologize sincerely when wrong.
9. Respect others' property.
10. Cover mouth when coughing.
11-50: Politeness opens doors. Always show gratitude, maintain eye contact, and honor school values.
